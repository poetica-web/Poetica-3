WHY WE GATHER

The Poetica Manifesto

01

No Pretense, Only Raw Drafts

We celebrate incomplete thoughts and unpolished couplets. You don't need a published book to take the chair; you only need a voice willing to linger in quiet honesty.

02

Candlelight & Open Cadence

Our bi-weekly meetups are structured around thematic prompts. Whether you choose spoken performance, soft reading, or silent listening, the space adapts to your rhythm.

03

The Quarterly Zine

Every three months, members curate and hand-press a limited physical poetry chapbook featuring selected verses born inside our circle.
